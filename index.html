How it works

1 · Choose a folder

Pick any folder on your computer, such as Downloads or a camera roll.

2 · See the groups

The AI looks at every photo and video and shows them grouped. Change any file you disagree with.

3 · Move, or undo

One click moves files into a Sorted folder. Not happy? Undo puts everything back.

Questions

Are my files uploaded?

No. The AI runs inside your browser on your own computer. Only a one-time download of the AI model, about 150 MB, happens the first time.

Which browser do I need?

Chrome on a computer (Mac, Windows or Linux). It needs Chrome's folder access, which phones and Safari don't have yet.

Can it delete my files?

No. It only moves files into a Sorted folder inside the folder you chose, and the Undo button puts them back.

Does it work on videos?

Yes. It looks at one frame of each video to decide where it belongs.

Your files never leave your computer. We only count anonymous visits.
